Instagram integration for the social media management framework of Odoo.

Connect an Instagram professional account (Business or Creator) to Odoo
through the *Instagram API with Instagram Login* and schedule image,
carousel and reel (video) posts.

**Table of contents**

.. contents::
   :local:

Usage
=====

To use this module you need a Meta app with the *Instagram* product and
the *Instagram Login* use case, and an Instagram professional account
(Business or Creator) to link it.

Go to *Social Media > Configuration > Social medias*, click *Associate
account* on Instagram and fill in your Instagram App ID and Instagram
App Secret.
